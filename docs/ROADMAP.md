# Roadmap

The roadmap is structured as a phased evolution from a secure business-management foundation into an **offline-first, data-driven, production-ready platform**.

## Month 1 — Platform Foundation

Establish the core platform with authentication, multi-tenancy, product catalogue, inventory management, customer management, dashboard capabilities, API documentation, containerization, and CI.

## Month 2 — Sales & Credit Management

Introduce sales workflows, sale line items, payment tracking, customer ledgers, debt settlement, receipt generation, and reporting.

## Month 3 — Offline-First Architecture

Enable reliable offline workflows through IndexedDB repositories, a synchronization outbox, idempotency keys, retry states, conflict-resolution policies, and synchronization UI.

## Month 4 — Logistics & Fulfilment

Add order management, delivery requests, rider management, assignment workflows, notifications, and real-time delivery status tracking.

## Month 5 — Intelligence & Analytics

Introduce a Python-based analytics service for demand forecasting and inventory anomaly detection, providing data-driven insights for business operations.

## Month 6 — Production Hardening

Strengthen the platform for production with refresh tokens, rate limiting, audit trails, metrics, distributed tracing, performance testing, deployment automation, and structured release processes.
